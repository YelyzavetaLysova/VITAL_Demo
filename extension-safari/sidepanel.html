How to read results

Supported and contradicted describe the retrieved evidence, not guaranteed truth. Insufficient evidence means no reliable assessment could be made; it does not mean false.

Topic, advertorial and sentiment scores are model outputs, not accuracy ratings. Local classifiers use a shortened article excerpt; sentiment is English-only. Publisher metadata does not determine whether a claim is correct.
