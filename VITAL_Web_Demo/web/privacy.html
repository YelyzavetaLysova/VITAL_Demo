Privacy and research use

Updated 28 October 2026 · Notice version 2026-10-28.1

Who is responsible

The University of Bergen, through SFI MediaFutures, is responsible for this application. For privacy questions or requests, email office@mediafutures.no. MediaFutures is located at Lars Hilles gate 30, 5008 Bergen, Norway.

What happens when you use VITAL

No account is required and we do not ask you to provide identifying information. Opening the extension extracts article text locally. Article content is uploaded only after you accept the notice and press Analyse.

The submitted information includes the article title, text, URL, language and available publication metadata. On the web application, adding an article link starts an import and analysis. Imported web articles and their results are shared with other visitors in the article catalogue. Extension submissions are not added to that public catalogue.

VITAL stores submitted article snapshots, analysis results and any additional passages you submit for checking. Source articles and comments can themselves contain information about people. We therefore do not describe all incoming content as anonymous.

Services receiving content

Content is handled by our application hosted on NREC and the VITAL API at api.vital.mediafutures.no. Factiverse receives claim content for verification when that function is available. No other content-analysis recipients are used by this application.

Importing a URL contacts the original publisher to retrieve its article and images. Opening an original article or evidence link takes you to that publisher's website, where its own privacy practices apply.

Scientific research and retention

Researchers may review individual submitted articles, analysis results and feedback, as well as aggregated results, to evaluate VITAL's accuracy, limitations and usefulness and improve automated news analysis.

Submitted records remain in the operational system until the research team reviews them. The team manually removes identifying information before placing data in long-term research storage. Anonymised research data is retained indefinitely. This is a manual process; the application does not automatically anonymise submissions.

Removing names alone may not make a record anonymous. Research copies must not retain identifiers or combinations of details that allow a person to be identified. Raw submissions and operational records are not described as permanently anonymous research data.

Optional feedback and browser storage

Feedback consists of your rating, optional comment, the related analysis or claim, the time of submission and a random installation identifier used to prevent duplicate feedback. Researchers may read individual feedback. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in comments.

Your browser stores appearance preferences, acceptance of this notice and feedback receipts. A random installation identifier is created when you first submit feedback. It is not an account, but can link feedback from the same browser before anonymisation.

The organizer area uses a session cookie for authentication. Network services necessarily receive connection information such as IP addresses; server and API logs may contain technical request data. These logs are operational records, not anonymous research results.

Your choice and rights

Participation and feedback are voluntary. We ask for your agreement before you submit content for analysis and research use. Where personal data is processed on the basis of your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

In Settings, Withdraw agreement stops new submissions from that browser until you agree again. It does not cancel a job already sent to the API, delete earlier records or notify the research team. Contact us if you want us to address an earlier submission.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability, and object to processing. Email office@mediafutures.no with the subject VITAL privacy request. Include an article URL, analysis identifier or other details that help locate the record; do not send sensitive identity documents unless specifically needed and requested. We normally respond within one month and explain any lawful extension or refusal.

You may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet. Rights are subject to applicable legal conditions. Irreversibly anonymised records can no longer be linked to an individual.

How to interpret results

Automated assessments can be wrong. Supported and contradicted describe the evidence retrieved for a claim. Insufficient evidence does not mean false. Source ratings describe a publisher, not the truth of each article. Framing and commercial-content classifications are model assessments. Check original sources. VITAL does not provide medical advice.

Chrome extension data use

Information obtained through the extension is used for the disclosed article-analysis features, optional feedback and evaluation of those features. It is not sold, used for advertising or used for unrelated profiling. Human review is limited to the research evaluation disclosed before submission. Our use of information obtained through the extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
