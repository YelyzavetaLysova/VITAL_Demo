Before you begin

Using VITAL

When you press Analyse, the article title, text and URL are sent to our NREC-hosted service and the VITAL API. Factiverse is used for claim verification when available.

Researchers at SFI MediaFutures, University of Bergen, may review submitted articles, results and optional feedback. Identifying information is removed manually before indefinite research storage.

No account is required. Please do not include personal information in feedback.

What the results mean

Automated assessments can be wrong. Insufficient evidence does not mean a claim is false. Check the linked sources. VITAL does not provide medical advice.
